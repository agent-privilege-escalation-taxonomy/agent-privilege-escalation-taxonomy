## Shortcut and literal controls

The policy was tuned on the development split only. The design avoids trace identifiers, exact attack payloads, exact tenant domain literals, and one-off generator fields. The test suite includes a literal guard over source string constants and a guard against large content keyword lists. The benchmark shortcut and overfit reports were reviewed; the final controls are based on privilege boundaries, requester authority, taint, structural validation, capability scopes, and egress flow rather than memorised benchmark strings.

## Formal model checker result

The deny-by-default model in `specs/DenyByDefault.tla` was checked with the Temporal Logic of Actions model checker on 2026-09-26. It found no invariant violation for `NoBypass`; 66 states were generated, 34 distinct states were found, zero states were left on the queue, and the complete search depth was 2.

## Interpretation

The strengthened policy improves from 70.2 percent to 96.4 percent attack blocking while keeping the false-positive rate at 0.8 percent. Remaining misses are concentrated in tool scope expansion and a small number of indirect prompt injection cases where the trace does not expose enough runtime provenance to distinguish the malicious step from an authorized delegated action without creating additional false positives.
